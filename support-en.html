Contact the PullX team

For a question, a bug report or a request about your data, write to the PullX team. You can write in English.

Open the contact form

Include your PullX version, iPhone or Apple Watch model, operating system version, what you expected and what happened. For a bug report, describe the steps that reproduce the problem and any error message.

Do not send passwords, Health documents or a full workout export for an initial enquiry. The form is hosted by Tally; your answers are sent to the PullX team and are not published here. Read the privacy policy before submitting.

Before reporting a problem

  • Check that PullX is up to date on iPhone and, if used, Apple Watch. Update both apps together.
  • If the watch no longer receives updates, check that the devices are within range and PullX is open on iPhone.
  • If the problem persists, describe whether it happens every time or only in certain circumstances. Avoid resetting your data while the issue is being investigated.

For workout setup, timers, Apple Watch and saving, see the guide.

Requests about your data

Use the same contact form for questions about privacy or to request access to, correction of or deletion of information submitted to the team.

Workout data on your devices is managed in PullX. Workouts already saved to Health must be deleted separately in Apple’s Health app. See the retention and deletion policy.
