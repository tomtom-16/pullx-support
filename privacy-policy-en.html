Contact

For questions about your data, contact the PullX team using the contact form.

How the app works locally

The current version of PullX works without an account, a PullX server, app-specific iCloud sync, advertising or built-in analytics. Workout data stays on your devices, with the exchanges needed between your iPhone and paired Apple Watch.

PullX does not automatically send your history, notes or health information to the developer. This data is neither sold nor used for advertising tracking.

Data stored on your devices

PullX stores the titles, exercises, instructions, settings, repetitions, durations and rest periods needed for your workouts. It may also store workout history, results, effort ratings, pain information and notes you choose to enter.

The active workout, a completed workout awaiting confirmation, and information needed to resume workouts and coordinate with your watch may also be stored. Local files benefit from the protections provided by Apple’s operating system.

Apple Watch and Health

On Apple Watch, PullX may request permission to write a strength training workout to Health. The current version does not request permission to read your Health data.

Saving is requested when you choose to finish and save the workout. Finishing without saving or discarding does not keep the current Health workout. A workout already saved to Health must be deleted separately in Apple’s Health app.

Exchanges between iPhone and Apple Watch

The iPhone keeps the authoritative workout and history. The watch receives its state and sends your actions. When the iPhone is temporarily unreachable, an action may wait for delivery and confirmation. No PullX server is involved.

Export, import and backups

You can export your data as JSON and choose where to save it or whom to share it with. The file may include your history, effort and pain information, and notes. Check its contents before sharing. PullX does not send it automatically.

Importing an export replaces local data after validation. Device backups and restores follow Apple’s settings and rules; restoring a backup may bring back previously saved data.

Retention and deletion

Local data is retained to track and resume your workouts until you delete or replace it in the app.

You can edit or delete workouts and delete history entries. Maintenance can reset the active workout, history and custom workouts, with separate options to reset settings. Default workouts are restored by a full reset.

Uninstalling removes local data according to iOS rules. Exports, device backups and workouts already saved to Health must be managed separately.

Purchases and free trial

Apple processes purchases and offer codes through the App Store. PullX checks verified transactions to recognize the full version unlock and calculate the seven-day trial from its original activation date. These transactions are not sent to a PullX server, and the developer does not receive your payment details. Resetting app data does not reset purchases or the trial associated with your Apple Account.

Support requests

If you contact the PullX team, your reply contact details and the information you submit become accessible to the team to handle your request. This is separate from the app’s local operation. Avoid sending health data or workout exports.

Requests are submitted through a form hosted by Tally. Data includes your reply address, subject and message, and any technical information you choose to provide. It is used to answer your request. It is accessible to the PullX team and processed by Tally to provide the form. If email notifications are enabled, a copy may also reach the team’s mailbox.

Requests and any email copies are retained for as long as needed to handle and follow up on them. You can use the same form to request access to, correction of or deletion of submitted information. These requests concern contact submissions; workout data stored on your devices is managed in PullX and Health.

Visiting these pages

These pages contain no analytics, embedded forms, remote fonts or advertising scripts. The Tally form opens only when you follow its link. The page host and Tally may process technical data needed to provide their services, including your IP address and browser information.

Provider information: Tally privacy policy. If you visit these pages on GitHub Pages, also see the GitHub privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current version of PullX. It will be updated if features, permissions, providers or data practices change. PullX is a fitness tool and does not provide medical diagnoses.
