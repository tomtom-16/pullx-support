Understanding PullX

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Build and follow strength and bodyweight workouts on iPhone, with controls on Apple Watch.

Home, Editor and History

Home starts a saved workout, Quick workout or Tabata. Editor creates, reorders and edits your workouts. History stores the workouts you choose to save, with their results. The gear button on Home opens Maintenance for purchases, export, import and resets.

Repetitions and timed exercises

PullX has two exercise types: Repetitions and Timed. The former Hold and EMOM types now share the Timed type; EMOM is a configuration shortcut.

Repetitions sets a goal of 1–200 reps without a timer. Validate the exercise manually when done. Timed sets a duration of 1–600 seconds, with an optional goal of 1–200 reps. Without a rep goal, it can represent a plank or another hold. With a rep goal, complete your reps and recover within the remaining time.

Exercise names and instructions are your own content. Changing the app language does not translate saved workouts or notes.

Starting timers and sound cues

Manual: tap Start each time the exercise is reached. Optional preparation runs before the exercise timer. Automatic: the exercise starts when reached, including the first automatic timer when you start the workout. Preparation runs only at the start of consecutive automatic exercises in the same block, including across repeated sets. A rest step, a manual exercise or a different block starts a new sequence.

Preparation can last up to 60 seconds and is added to the exercise duration. A final 3, 2, 1 countdown can be enabled, with an early warning from 0–60 seconds and a periodic cue from 0–300 seconds. Zero disables a warning or periodic cue. The warning is limited to the timer duration. Coincident cues play once, with countdown taking priority over the warning, then the periodic cue. These cues do not change the workout sequence.

Exercise countdown settings also apply to its preparation. Rest countdowns are independent. Verify sound with your iPhone in the foreground; background audio and system surfaces have their own operating system constraints.

Rest and repeat blocks

Add rest steps lasting 5–600 seconds. To have no rest, omit the rest step. A repeat block repeats its exercises and rest steps for 1–99 sets. Blocks cannot contain another repeat block. A rest step inside a block also runs on the last set.

EMOM and automatic intervals

In a repeat block, + EMOM creates a timed exercise with 60 seconds, 5 reps, automatic start, 5 seconds of initial preparation and the final countdown enabled. All settings remain editable. The shortcut is available inside repeat blocks. In the exercise form and Quick workout, choose Timed and Automatic to configure the same behavior. Ten sets make a ten-minute EMOM, plus initial preparation. Complete your reps, then recover until the next minute starts.

For a different duration, use an automatic interval: for example, 90 seconds. Multiple automatic exercises can follow each other from the first set. Do not add a rest step between intervals unless you want extra rest and preparation to restart afterwards.

Quick workout

Choose an exercise, a rep goal or timer, and a number of sets. Rest between sets is a separate choice: enabled by default with manual starts and disabled with automatic starts. You can change it. Quick workout and Editor keep separate presets, which you can reset in Maintenance.

Tabata

Tabata alternates work and rest with an initial preparation period. The free version provides 8 rounds of 20 seconds of work and 10 seconds of rest, with 10 seconds of preparation. PullX Complete allows customization of rounds, cycles, work, rest, preparation, extra rest between cycles and whether to include the final rest. Extra cycle rest is added to the normal rest after a cycle’s last round, except in the final cycle.

During a workout

Follow the current exercise, instructions, timer and progress. You can pause, resume, restart or skip a timer and return to the previous step. Timers use deadlines and catch up after suspension. During a rest, enter or correct the previous exercise’s reps or seconds, effort, optional pain information and notes. A timed exercise with a rep goal can retain both reps and duration; PullX does not count reps automatically.

Finishing, history and Health

At the end, review the step summary and choose Finish and save or Finish without saving. Saved sessions appear in History, where you can copy a text report or delete a session. A stopped workout can also be reviewed.

On Apple Watch, Health saving requires permission and an explicit save decision. PullX does not read your Health data. Finishing without saving or discarding does not save the current Health workout. Deleting PullX history does not remove a workout already saved to Health.

Apple Watch and Live Activity

Start on the paired iPhone and open PullX on the watch. The iPhone keeps the authoritative state and history; Watch actions need its confirmation. Keep both apps updated. A Live Activity can show the current step, countdown and progress on supported system surfaces. This display does not replace the app’s finish and save decision.

AI import and backups

In Editor, copy the AI prompt, give it to your chosen AI service with your workout request, then paste the returned JSON into Import an AI workout. Check the preview before adding it. The English prompt asks for English names and instructions. For EMOM, specify the number of sets and reps per minute: the current PullX v2 prompt creates automatic timed exercises in a repeat block. Copy the current prompt again if you used an older version. AI output must follow PullX’s format and exercise limits; it can be rejected when invalid.

Maintenance exports a JSON backup and can import a previous export. Importing a maintenance export replaces local data after validation. Keep a recent backup before replacing data. Exports can contain personal notes and pain information; check them before sharing.

Free version, full version and trial

The free version includes a quick workout of up to 2 sets, basic Tabata and one custom workout with up to 3 steps. Manual timed exercises, with or without a rep goal, are available within these limits; automatic starts require full access. The Watch app, history, export and deletion remain available. PullX Complete is a one-time purchase, with no subscription or ads, unlocking additional workouts and steps, repeat blocks, automatic intervals and full Tabata settings.

The optional full-access trial lasts 7 consecutive days from activation, with no automatic charge. When it ends, the free limits apply again and your data stays saved. The full version price is shown in the app. Restore purchases uses the Apple Account’s verified transactions; resetting app data does not restart the trial.

Language and support

On iPhone, select Settings → Apps → PullX → Language → English. Add English under General → Language & Region first if needed. You can keep your iPhone and region set to French and France. Apple Watch has separate language settings in the Watch app on iPhone.

PullX is a fitness tracking tool and does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional healthcare advice.
